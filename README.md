## 👋 About Me

I'm **Supriya**, a **UX/UI & Product Designer with a frontend development background**, based in Bengaluru.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **design, technology, and product thinking** — understanding a problem, shaping the experience, designing the interface, and knowing enough code to make the idea real.

My focus is on creating **simple, useful, scalable digital products** rather than designing screens in isolation.

### What I enjoy

- 🧠 Product thinking & problem solving
- 🎨 UX/UI & visual design
- 🤖 AI product experiences
- 📊 SaaS & data-heavy interfaces
- 📱 Mobile & responsive experiences
- 🧩 Design systems & reusable components
- 💻 Frontend implementation

## 🎯 My Design Approach

```
Understand
    ↓
Research & Define
    ↓
Explore Ideas
    ↓
Wireframe
    ↓
Prototype
    ↓
Test & Refine
    ↓
Design System
    ↓
Build & Iterate
```

I care about **why something should exist**, not only how it should look.

---

## ⚡ Design Principles

| Principle | How I approach it |
|---|---|
| 🎯 Clarity | Make complex information easy to understand |
| 👤 User-first | Design around real user needs and behaviours |
| 🧩 Consistency | Build reusable patterns and design systems |
| ♿ Accessibility | Make experiences usable by more people |
| 📐 Scalability | Design systems that can grow with the product |
| 💻 Feasibility | Consider implementation while designing |
